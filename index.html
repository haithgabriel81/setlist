Where the ratings come from

Overall uses 50% talent, 30% record performance, and 20% streaming reach. Talent is an editorial game score reflecting technique, songwriting/production, versatility, and performance; it is subjective, not an objective ranking. Commercial scores use logarithmic scaling so a huge audience does not make everyone else irrelevant. When data is missing, known weights are renormalized. Artists with no commercial figures have a clearly marked provisional talent-only overall. Unknown figures are never entered as zero. Draft cards replace missing commercial figures with Stage appeal and Creativity: derived game attributes, not measured artist statistics. Real commercial figures remain available in the artist archive.

Streaming figures are Spotify lead-artist totals reported by ChartMasters on September 29, 2026. Records measure estimated non-stream equivalent album units (total EAS minus stream EAS) from ChartMasters, September 28, 2026; they are not pure album sales or official certifications. These are snapshots, not live counters. Festival budget estimates come from a fictional planning formula based on game ratings and booking categories, with a 35% production allowance plus $300,000 and a ±30% range. They are not verified booking fees. Read about slayr on SoundCloud.

The starting roster contains 80 selected artists, not every artist in existence. Discovery is a game-booking category, not a claim that every act is still unknown. This is a fantasy, cross-era festival, including artists who are deceased. Audience, reviews, and buzz are simulated game results, not forecasts or real-world statistics. Two-player mode runs on the same device. Daily challenges reset at midnight UTC and use seeded choices with standard rating rules. Saved lineups, themes, and test stats stay in this browser; no data is sent to an analytics service. Sharing a lineup link includes the festival name, artist picks, and game score.
